## Brand & Style

This design system channels an uncompromising aesthetic of silent luxury, quiet elegance, and editorial discipline. Drawing inspiration from high-fashion maisons and artisanal botanicals—reminiscent of The Row, Aesop, and Kinfolk—the interface recedes entirely into the background to allow the raw, natural amber tones and tactile glass packaging of the apiculture harvest to lead the experience. 

The emotional target is sophisticated, deliberate, and deeply grounded in provenance and single-harvest scarcity. The UI treats commerce as an art publication: dense commercial banners, aggressive call-to-actions, and distracting badges are replaced by architectural alignment, whisper-quiet micro-details, hyper-refined hairline rules, and expansive negative space. The interaction model is calm, measured, and tactile, evoking high-touch apothecary craftsmanship rooted in Saitabat since 1985.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an organic, museum-grade chromatic structure:

- **Primary (`#141414`)**: A soft soot-charcoal black. Pure `#000000` is strictly avoided to preserve organic warmth across typography and primary structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#C9A961`) & Named Honey Amber (`#D9A342`)**: Reserved with extreme restraint. This warm golden tincture is used exclusively for pricing numerals, delicate botanical provenance stamps, active navigation indicators, and focus rings. It is never applied to large surface fills.
- **Neutral (`#FAFAF8`) & Canvas Cream (`#F4F0E8`)**: An unbleached, raw linen off-white background that reduces harsh screen glare and provides an editorial foundation for rich product photography.
- **Hairlines & Dividers (`#E8E8E4` / `#8C8C8C`)**: Ultra-delicate architectural lines measuring 0.5px to 1px that frame imagery and define zones without adding visual density.
- **Tertiary (`#737370`)**: A calm, balanced stone-gray dedicated to secondary product specs, vintage years, harvest notes, and subtle category metadata.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual architecture of this design system, balancing austere geometric modernity with laboratory-precise technical documentation.

- **Headlines & Primary Display (`DM Sans`)**: Rendered in light (`300`) and regular (`400`) weights. Letterforms remain uncluttered, open, and confident without theatrical ornament.
- **Narrative & Body Text (`Inter`)**: Set with generous leading (1.7 to 1.8 ratio) to promote thoughtful, immersive reading across provenance narratives and clinical cosmetic benefits.
- **Micro-Labels, Navigation, & Category Identifiers**: Set in all-caps with intentional letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.15em`), evoking European luxury editorial catalogs.
- **Pricing & Chemical/Botanical Specs (`JetBrains Mono`)**: The inclusion of a monospaced typeface honors the scientific background of Dr. Şenol, providing clinical rigor to batch numbers, harvest elevations, and apothecary volumes (e.g., `500G / 17.6 OZ`, `ALTITUDE: 1200M`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout draws directly from high-fashion visual lookbooks and curated editorial feeds:

- **Grid Model**: A hybrid 12-column desktop framework that transitions into an Instagram-inspired modular image grid for product curation. Product feeds use compact, intentional gutters (`0.5rem` to `1rem`), keeping full-bleed product imagery tight, seamless, and dominant.
- **Aspect Ratios**: Strictly anchored to vertical portrait (`3:4` or `4:5`) and classical square (`1:1`). Media blocks never use decorative rounded clipping.
- **Rhythm & White Space**: Macro layouts alternate between dense visual product mosaics and expansive, generous white-space breathing rooms (`space-xl` and above) reserved for editorial harvest essays and single-origin testimonials.
- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): 2-column seamless product grid, `1rem` outer canvas margin, and slim navigation.
  - Tablet (`768px - 1024px`): 3-column structured grid with `2rem` outer margins.
  - Desktop (`> 1024px`): 4-column product grid or 12-column editorial showcase with fixed max-width bounding containers (`1440px`) and `3rem` margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system is intentionally flat and physical, rejecting drop shadows, heavy blur spreads, and synthetic skeuomorphic overlays:

- **Surface Strategy**: Layering is achieved solely via tonal shifts between the base canvas (`#FAFAF8`), slight surface contrast panels (`#F4F0E8`), and crisp hairline borders (`#E8E8E4`).
- **Hairline Rules**: Visual separation relies on 0.5px (rendered as true single sub-pixel) and 1px linear partitions. Borders delineate cards and list items like editorial column dividers in a print periodical.
- **No Drop Shadows**: Cards, drawers, and buttons sit flush on the canvas with `box-shadow: none`. 
- **Modals & Overlays**: When a drawer or modal emerges (e.g., Quick Bag or Botanical Index), a sheer, neutral dim veil (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.25)`) is used with zero backdrop blur, ensuring absolute fidelity to raw image tones underneath.

## Shapes

The geometry of this design system is architectural, deliberate, and sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Every structural component—including imagery, interactive buttons, modal sheets, notification bars, and input surfaces—features precise, unrounded 90-degree corners. 
- Form follows editorial paper prints, linen boxes, and pharmaceutical glass packaging. 
- The only natural curves present in the viewport originate organically from within the photography itself (the curvature of honey jars, droplet viscous textures, and bee hives).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid soot-charcoal (`#141414`) fill with neutral off-white (`#FAFAF8`) text. Sharp edges, no shadow. Height 48px, compact horizontal padding (24px). Text styled in `nav-caps` (all-caps, tracking `0.12em`). Subtle hover transition: background dissolves softly to `#2A2A28` over 250ms.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action**: Transparent fill with a 1px solid hairline (`#141414` or `#E8E8E4`). Text in soft black. On hover, inverts gracefully with pure hairline retention.
- **Text Action**: Raw text button accompanied by a single 1px underline positioned 4px below the baseline, shifting to `#D9A342` on hover.

### Product Cards (Instagram Editorial Style)
- **Container**: Borderless or framed with a razor-thin 0.5px `#E8E8E4` border. 
- **Visual Display**: Image-first presentation in 3:4 portrait ratio. Hover reveals a secondary lifestyle shot with an imperceptible fade-in.
- **Card Metadata**: Padded underneath with minimal vertical gap (`space-sm`). Contains category badge in `editorial-caption`, product name in `body-sm`, and price in `price-tag` (`JetBrains Mono`, colored in `#141414` or highlighted in `#D9A342`).

### Chips & Filters
- **Form**: Rectangular, flat, border 1px solid `#E8E8E4`, no border-radius.
- **States**: Unselected chips feature `#FAFAF8` background with `#737370` text. Selected chips shift to a solid `#141414` background with `#FAFAF8` typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Flat, minimal bottom-border-only variant (1px `#E8E8E4`) or full perimeter box. Zero background fill. Placeholder set in muted `#737370` using `body-md`. Focus state transitions bottom border to `#141414` or subtle `#D9A342` without expanding outline rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Strict square inputs with 1px hairline perimeter. Checked state displays an inner solid charcoal micro-square with a 3px inset margin.

### Navigation Header
- **Layout**: Fixed or subtle sticky header with broken-white background (`#FAFAF8`) and bottom 0.5px divider `#E8E8E4`.
- **Elements**: Centered or left-aligned architectural typography mark "DR. ŞENOL", balanced by widely tracked navigation links (`nav-caps`) and right-aligned minimal utilities: `SEARCH`, `ACCOUNT`, `BAG [0]`.

### Editorial Narrative Blocks
- **Format**: Alternating full-width text-and-imagery sections highlighting Saitabat provenance, harvesting dates, and lab-tested pollen profiles. Text uses an oversized serif or DM Sans display hero headline juxtaposed with narrow, high-legibility Inter body paragraphs.
